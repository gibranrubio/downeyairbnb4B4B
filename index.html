Amenities

AC/Heat
-Feel free to set your preferred temperature. We do ask to please turn it off when not in the house and if you would kindly not go below 69 degrees/above 81 degrees.Towels
- If you need fresh towels, please feel free to use the washer/dryer.Soap and Shampoo
-Please call us if you run out. We will get some to you immediately!Toilet Paper
-We have extra under the sink in each bathroom. Please call us if you run out. We will get some to you immediately!Washer/Dryer
-Please feel free to use as you need and please note we do provide a tide pod per stay.Coffee
-Coffee pods are under the Keurig machine.Blow Dryer
-Located under the full bathroom sink.Clothes Iron
-Located in the middle closet of the hallway to the bedrooms. Please use a towel over the bedsheets if you need to iron.TV
-We have smart TVs in every bedroom and living room. Each room has Netflix signed in for you to click on the Airbnb account to use.Fireplace
-This is a gas fireplace that needs a key to turn on the gas then apply a flame from a lighter. Please use as you'd like and turn off when not using it. Be very cautious as the flame lights quickly if you release too much gas out. Please do not ever use any real firewood inside as this will cause damages.Trash Cans
-If you need to throw trash out or have food containers, please place in the black trash container in the backyard next to the wooden gate. Please try to recycle which is the blue trash bin. Any glass or metal cans can be placed in the wooden crate on the back patio.
